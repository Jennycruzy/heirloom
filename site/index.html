The blind spot

Every check was green.
Six workflows were still wrong.

The first modern implementation passed all of its application tests and every structural parity check: every field present, every length exact, every task on the menu. Reading the COBOL behind the screens showed that customer and policy numbers were typed by the clerk instead of assigned by the system, updates skipped the retrieve-then-edit sequence, and motor policies could be reached without the customer who holds them.

Method

Machines for facts. Models only for meaning.

Anything that can be compared exactly is compared by a deterministic check. Model judgement is reserved for workflow meaning, must cite source lines, and is allowed to answer cannot determine. Nothing a model says counts as proof by itself.

  1. 01

    Read the pinned source

    BMS maps and COBOL programs from IBM's GenApp at a pinned commit. The mainframe is never run, compiled or emulated.

    Source
  2. 02

    Catalogue every screen

    6 screens, 20 visible actions: 18 confirmed by map and program, 2 recorded as cannot determine. 20/20 validation checks.

    Bob · validated
  3. 03

    Rebuild the green screens

    Each map is recomposed from the catalogue onto an exact 24×80 grid and tested cell by cell.

    Deterministic
  4. 04

    Modernize seven tasks

    Customer inquire, add, update; motor policy inquire, add, delete, update. Python standard library, SQLite, no frameworks.

    Build
  5. 05

    Compare exact facts

    Operations, fields, maximum lengths, numeric flags and form controls: 14 checks, no model involved.

    Deterministic
  6. 06

    Review workflow meaning

    Separate Bob reviewers per transaction read the COBOL behind each task. An evidence audit checks every claim against the cited lines.

    Model · cited
  7. 07

    Repair only what is proven

    Each fix needs a check that fails before and passes after. A final independent review returned READY.

    Fail → pass

Evidence boundary

What Heirloom refuses to claim.

A parity tool that guesses is worse than none. Where the source does not settle a question, the answer is recorded as cannot determine and left open.

  • The mainframe was never run

    Every legacy claim comes from BMS and COBOL source at a pinned commit. Nothing was compiled, emulated or executed.

  • Every record is invented

    The demo customers and policies are fictional test data created for this project, never presented as GenApp or client data.

How AI was used

Reviewers, not oracles.

IBM Bob
Planned the source extraction, launched separate customer and motor-policy reviewers to read the COBOL behind each task, and independently reviewed the repaired branch against cited source, tests and browser evidence before returning READY.
watsonx.ai Granite
Supplied documented fallback drafts for the screen reconstruction while Bob was reserved for evidence-heavy review. Small runtime and accessibility defects in those drafts were corrected and tested before the work was accepted.
Deterministic checks and people
Verified everything the models produced. When a Bob reviewer stalled, its report was kept unchanged and the gaps were completed by a separate evidence audit rather than presented as finished.
